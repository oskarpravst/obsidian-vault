# 2026-09-22 - Introduction to Academic Studies

## Main Idea

The main idea of [[Programming|programming]] is turning a [[Problem|problem]] into an [[Algorithm|algorithm]] and then into a [[Program|program]].

## Notes

A human's natural language is connected with the [[Computer|computer]]'s [[Binary language|binary language]] with a [[Compiler|compiler]]. 

[[Programming]] is just the motion of transforming natural language into something a computer understands([[Programming Language|programming language]]).

There are many stages of a computer program:
1. simple and stupid(repetitive)
2. adding [[For Loop|for loops]](a little less repetitive), adding [[Variable|variables]] at the start.
3. Adding [[Function|functions]] if there are more parts

There are more levels of programming:
1. how can a robot move its hand
2. what can the robot do

During writing our programs we must keep in mind the speed of the program. If it is not optimized it will affect the speed of execution.

Frequency and core number of the [[Processor|processor]] affect the speed of execution as well.

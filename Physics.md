## Exam

1st colloquium (*1. kolokvij*): **2026-11-25**, 5 problems in 90 min. Covers everything up to then (lectures 1–7 and the matching exercises).

### Topics

- Kinematics: 1D motion, graphs, relative velocity, projectile motion, circular motion — [[2026-10-11 - Physical Quantities and Kinematics - 1]], [[2026-10-13 - Circular Motion and Newton's Laws - 2]]
- Newton's laws, friction, inclines — [[2026-10-13 - Circular Motion and Newton's Laws - 2]]
- Work, power, energy; gravitation — [[2026-10-20 - Forces, Work, Energy and Momentum - 3]]
- Momentum, collisions, rocket — [[2026-10-27 - Systems of Bodies, Collisions and Rotation - 4]]
- Rigid bodies: torque, moment of inertia, rolling, pulleys — [[2026-10-27 - Systems of Bodies, Collisions and Rotation - 4]]
- Angular momentum, Kepler's laws, oscillations — [[2026-11-03 - Angular Momentum, Kepler's Laws, Precession and Oscillations - 5]]

### Typical problems

Based on the 1st colloquia 2014–2023 (6 papers, 30 problems). Each paper roughly has:

1. **Kinematics** (6/6 papers): read a $v(t)$ graph → acceleration, distance, sketch $x(t)$ (2019, 2023); meeting / overtaking; crossing a river; fastest route (2022).
2. **Collision / momentum** (5/6): perfectly inelastic collision in 2D (2019 train and cannonball, 2023 Titanic), energy lost in the collision, impulse.
3. **Rigid body** (5/6): pulley with mass + blocks on an incline with friction (2022, 2023), yo-yo (2019), rolling (2017), statics of a rod (2014, 2022).
4. **Gravitation** (4 papers in a row, 2019–2023): $g$ on another planet, orbit radius from the period, escape velocity, an unusual force law, gravity of a rod (integral).
5. **Angular momentum** (4/6): pirouette and the work done by the skater (2019), rod + snowball (2017), hamster wheel (2014), spaceship + asteroid (2021).
6. **Oscillations** (3/6): physical pendulum, period after a collision (2017), pendulum + spring (2021).
7. **Variable force → integrate / differential equation** (2/6): friction $k(t) = \beta t$ (2023), constant power $v(t)$ (2021).

## Readings

- A. Franc, D. Franetič: [Rešene naloge iz diskretnih struktur, 2026](http://matematika.fri.uni-lj.si/ds/dszbirka.pdf). (Alfa verzija zbirke nalog iz Diskretnih struktur. To gradivo ni recenzirano in verjetno so se v rešitve nalog prikradle napake. Če mislite, da je kakšna rešitev napačna, ste vabljeni, da se oglasite na govorilnih urah ali po e-pošti.)
- V. Batagelj, S. Klavžar: DS1 - Logika in množice, Naloge, DMFA Ljubljana, 1997 (z rešitvami, poglavja 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, izjavni in predikatni račun, množice, relacije, funkcije).
- V. Batagelj, S. Klavžar: DS2 - Algebra in teorija grafov, Naloge, DMFA Ljubljana, 2000 (z rešitvami, poglavja o permutacijah (3) in teoriji grafov (6,7,8)).

**Comprehensive monograph (several copies available in local library, unfortunately not open access)**

- James Hein. Discrete Structures, Logic and computability, Jones and Bartlett. ([study guide](http://samples.jbpub.com/9780763772062/Revised_SSG.pdf)) [JH_GUIDE]

**Half-open access.**

- Gordon J. Pace. [Mathematics of Discrete Structures for Computer Science](http://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-642-29840-0), Springer, 2012. [GP_MDSCS]

**Wikibook.**

- [Discrete Mathematics, Wikibook](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Discrete_Mathematics). [WBOOK]  
    

**Further online readings.**

- Vladlen Koltun. [Discrete Structures, Lecture Notes](http://web.stanford.edu/class/cs103x/cs103x-notes.pdf), Stanford, 2008. [VK_DSLN]
- Rafael Pass, Wei-Lung, Dustin Tseng. [A Course in Discrete Structures](http://www.cs.cornell.edu/~rafael/discmath.pdf), Cornell. [RP_CDS]
